XoYokeBridge - Hardware that connects to Logitech Yoke

The XoYokeBridge hardware-and-firmware system empowers you to use a Logitech flight yoke, throttle quadrant, and rudder pedals to control Microsoft Flight Simulator on an Xbox ecosystem. The XoYokeBridge hardware unit connects directly to your Logitech equipment, instantly recognizing the yoke, switches, and pedal inputs to read your movements with precision. Operating over your home Wi-Fi network, it automatically locates your network's computer to beam your real-time control data across the room with near-zero latency, using integrated indicator lights to display streaming status, automatically recovering from temporary network drops, and featuring a built-in setup interface for effortless network updates.

XoYokeBridge v1.0 - Software

Seamlessly map your custom hardware input data directly into the Xbox ecosystem using an automated local virtualization layer.

The XoYokeBridge software receives this wireless data stream, cleans and calibrates the control signals, and converts them into a virtual Xbox controller format. The Xbox app or Microsoft Flight Simulator then reads the flight hardware as a native, fully responsive game controller. In simple terms, the entire system acts like a high-speed wireless translator that captures commands from your flight controls and instantly converts them into standard Xbox inputs, allowing you to fly your aircraft without long, messy USB cables.
